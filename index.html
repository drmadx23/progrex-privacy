Progrex Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Progrex ("the app", "we", "us") is a task and reminder app published by the app's developer. Progrex is built to work entirely on your own device, with no account and no server behind it. This page explains what information the app touches, where it's stored, and the choices you have.

Data stored only on your device

Everything you put into Progrex stays on your phone, in the app's private local storage. This includes:

  • Your tasks: titles, notes, due dates, estimates, priority, energy level, recurrence settings
  • Completion history: when a task was completed, how long it actually took, postpone history and the reasons you gave
  • Your "plan" fields on a task, if you fill them in: an implementation-intention trigger ("when X"), a next physical action, a precommitment choice
  • Gamification data: points, level, streaks, badges — calculated on-device from your task history
  • App preferences: theme, language, default time budget, energy preference, protected rest-time window, in-app language selection

We don't have access to any of this. It is never transmitted to us, to any server, or to any third party. There is no account to create and no sign-in of any kind in Progrex.

Calendar access (optional)

If you turn on calendar sync in Settings, Progrex writes an event to a calendar on your device (via Android's standard Calendar provider) for each task with a due date, and keeps that event updated as you edit or complete the task. This is opt-in, off by default, and entirely local to your device — Progrex only writes to your device's own calendar storage, the same as any other calendar app you already use, and does not read your existing events. Turning sync off leaves any already-created events in place; you can delete them yourself.

Notifications

Progrex schedules local reminder notifications for your tasks, based on the reminder lead time you choose per task (e.g. 15 minutes before, 1 day before). These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android's own notification and background-work systems. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Backup and export

Progrex includes a manual Backup/Export feature that lets you save a copy of your data (for example, to move it to a new device). This export is created and shared entirely at your own initiative, through your device's own file/share picker — we never receive a copy, and nothing is uploaded automatically.

No accounts, no ads, no purchases, no third-party analytics

Progrex has no login, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and does not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own.

Data deletion

Since all of your data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it completely. Within the app, you can also delete individual tasks or completed-task history directly from the Completed tab at any time.

Children's privacy

Progrex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children — in practice, we don't collect personal information from anyone, since nothing leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
